05 · Topic Drilldown · concept

A board drawn in code, playing the variation

The hatched board turned to mud in dark. Drawn in the app's own colours it is crisp in both themes and plays the mate the 14 October rule gives each theme, the army at the viewer's edge delivering it: light, Scholar's Mate from White's side (1.e4 e5 2.Bc4 Nc6 3.Qh5 d6 4.Qxf7#); dark, Fool's Mate from Black's side (1.f3 e5 2.g4 Qh4#). The game is played from the opening position in four beats, and on each beat the board says one thing Topic Drilldown does; on the mating move the queen's square lights, her path draws, the square she lands on holds, the line she or her bishop holds lights square by square, and the king's square glows last. Then the floor breathes: a few tiles at a time warm faintly and fade.
