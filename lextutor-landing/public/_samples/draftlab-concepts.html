Draft Lab review · concepts

A · Schematic

The draft annotated like a technical drawing. Every component carries a dimension bracket with its name and status; notes are numbered balloons. One large panel shows the note you are on and changes as you scroll or touch a balloon. Nothing stacks.

Lexa · Sound · 1 to correct · 2 to strengthen

B · Proof index

A live index of all thirteen components beside the page, each with its status. Touch a row to go to it. Corrections open inside the document as before and after, with the reason under them.

13 components · 1 to correct · 2 to strengthen

C · Spotlight

A guided review. The page dims and the part under review lights; its note stands beside it. Step with the arrows or the arrow keys; the review feels like a walk-through with a partner.

Phone · tap to view

The page stays clean. Each part with a note ends in a mark: solid for a correction, outlined for a point to strengthen. A mark invites the tap once as it comes into view, and the note unfolds under its part when tapped. The counts at the top take you to the first of each.
