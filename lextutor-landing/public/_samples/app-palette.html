What a real repaint also needs

  • The top bar and side rail are painted outside the colour tokens, so they stay near-black on green and white on bone here. A repaint brings them onto the tokens.
  • Optional columns in the graph stay neutral grey; on green they would take a green-grey so the gold reads cleaner.
  • The engraved plates on the home and workspace covers are drawn for black and paper; they would need green and bone grounds (or the founder's newer art).
  • Lexa's marks (strike, insert, develop, good) would be re-tuned and contrast-checked on each ground before shipping.
