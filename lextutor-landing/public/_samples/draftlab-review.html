Lexa

The motion on notice cannot stand as drafted: the authority recited is wrong in form, and the grounds for the application have not been drafted at all. The prayer and the service endorsement are also too thin to act on, and must be redrawn in the language of an order the registry can issue and an address the bailiff can use.

Lexa's notes

01Correct · Authority clause

State the rule that gives the court power to hear this application; the inherent jurisdiction alone cannot ground an interlocutory injunction. Recite Order 43 Rule 1 of the Lagos Rules 2019, adding the inherent jurisdiction if you wish.

Order 43 Rule 1(1), High Court of Lagos State (Civil Procedure) Rules 2019: a motion shall state the Rule of Court or Law under which the application is brought.
02Strengthen · Reliefs

Pray for the order in words the registry can draw up: restrain the Respondent, by itself, its agents, privies and assigns, from selling, transferring, leasing or otherwise parting with possession of the property, pending the hearing and determination of this suit.

03Strengthen · For service on

Name the Respondent and give an address at which service can be effected; as it stands, the bailiff has nothing to act on.

  • Judicial divisionYou have named the judicial division, which the usual form of a motion requires.
  • Holden atYou have given the place of sitting, and Lagos is a seat of the division you named.
  • Suit numberYou have carried the suit number from the originating process.
  • PartiesYou have titled the cause in the usual way, giving each party its capacity.
  • Opening clauseYour opening clause follows the usual words of motion practice.
